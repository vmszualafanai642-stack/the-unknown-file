CASE FILE #001

The House With No Owner

For twenty years, an old house at the edge of town has been abandoned. No family has officially lived there since 2006.

But every night at exactly 2:17 AM, the upstairs bedroom light turns on.

Three people claim they know nothing about the house. One of them is lying.

Your mission

Examine the evidence, investigate the suspects, solve the hidden code, and identify the person responsible.

Evidence Room

Evidence #01 — Photograph

A photograph taken at 2:20 AM shows the upstairs window. The light is clearly visible.

Evidence #02 — Footprints

Fresh footprints were found outside the back door. They lead toward the house, but none lead away from it.

Evidence #03 — Old Newspaper

A newspaper from 2006 reports that the previous owner disappeared without explanation.

Evidence #04 — Clock

An old wall clock inside the house is permanently stopped at exactly 2:17.

Evidence #05 — Note

A handwritten note was found underneath the floorboards. It contains only three numbers: 2 - 1 - 7

Suspect Files

Suspect A — Daniel Vane

A former police officer who investigated the disappearance in 2006. He claims he has not visited the house since then.

Suspect B — Clara Reed

A journalist researching the original disappearance. She says she has never entered the house.

Suspect C — Marcus Hale

The current owner of the land. He says the house has been completely empty for twenty years.

Hidden Code

The note found under the floorboards says:

2 - 1 - 7

The clock stopped at 2:17. The bedroom light appears at 2:17. The note contains 2, 1 and 7.

Combine the numbers into the three-digit code.


Final Accusation

You have examined the evidence. Who do you believe is responsible?
